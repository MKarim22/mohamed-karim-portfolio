About

Three layers, one strategist.

I'm a Marketing Manager & Creative Strategist with more than two decades of experience in advertising, branding, and digital marketing across Kuwait, Egypt and the wider Middle East.

My edge is in ROI-focused performance marketing — GA4, GTM, HubSpot, and automation workflows — paired with the eye of a creative director who has shaped brands from the canvas to the campaign.

Today I'm channelling that experience into something larger: building intelligent systems that don't just assist marketing teams, but run the loop — create, publish, respond, and sell.
